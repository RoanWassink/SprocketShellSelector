Sprocket Shell Selector v0.11.0

Close Sprocket. Copy the BepInEx folder into the folder containing Sprocket.exe and merge folders, replacing the old SprocketShellSelector.dll. Keep only one DLL copy.

Requires Sprocket 0.2.55.5 and BepInEx 6 IL2CPP (verified 6.0.0-be.788). Loader not included. Start the loader once before installing. Back up vehicle saves and previous mod files.

Examples are separate; do not copy default-shells.json over your installed custom catalog unless you intentionally want to replace your custom shells. Existing profiles/config/WAV overrides are preserved by the package.

To apply new stock-style motor tuning to an old ATGM profile, edit that profile with the game closed: launchSpeed=50, acceleration=150, motorDelay=0.12, motorBurnTime=4, coastDeceleration=1, flightSpeed=200. Keep its other required fields and identity. Gun-launched examples instead use launchSpeedMode=cannon, launchSpeedMultiplier=0.2 and motorDelay=0.08. Full examples/ranges are in CUSTOM-SHELLS.md.

Burn time 0 means unlimited burn; missing burn/coast fields keep legacy behavior. Only the newest missile per vehicle is guided. Custom WAV overrides take priority over the embedded sounds. Move/back up an old override to hear the bundled audio.

Rollback may require restoring both the previous DLL and matching shell JSON/CFG backups. Before uninstalling select vanilla ammunition on affected cannons and save, then remove only this mod's DLL. Keep configuration/audio backups and unrelated mods.
